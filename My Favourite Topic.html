The Art of Specialty Coffee

Specialty coffee is not just a drink; it is a craft that involves meticulous care from farm to cup. Understanding brew variables like grind size and water temperature brings out unique flavour notes.

Essential Brewing Equipment

To brew a clean and aromatic cup at home, having the right gear makes a significant difference. Here are the tools and basic steps used in manual brewing.

Required Tools

  • Manual burr grinder
  • Pour-over dripper (V60)
  • Paper filters
  • Digital scale with timer

Basic Pour-Over Steps

  1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water.
  2. Add ground coffee and tare the scale.
  3. Pour 50ml of hot water for blooming and wait 30 seconds.
  4. Pour the remaining water in continuous concentric circles.
